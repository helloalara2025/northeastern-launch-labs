# Northeastern Launch Labs - Brand Identity

## Design Philosophy
Inspired by Figma's playful, scrappy aesthetic. Bold, confident, approachable. Not corporate—feels like a startup built by students, for students.

## Color Palette

### Primary Color
- **Vibrant Purple**: `#7C3AED` - Bold, energetic, represents creativity and collaboration

### Supporting Colors
- **Charcoal**: `#1F2937` - Deep, professional text
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` - Clean backgrounds
- **Light Purple**: `#EDE9FE` - Subtle backgrounds and accents
- **Accent Orange**: `#F97316` - Secondary highlight for energy

## Typography System

### Font Pairing
- **Display/Headings**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Bold, Semibold)
- **Body/UI**: Inter (Regular, Medium, Semibold)

## Visual Language

### Design Principles
1. **Scrappy & Playful**: Imperfect, hand-drawn feel
2. **Bold & Confident**: Large typography, strong hierarchy
3. **Minimalist**: Remove clutter, embrace whitespace
4. **Cohesive**: All visual elements work together as a system

### Visual Motifs
- **Geometric shapes**: Circles, squares, lines used consistently
- **Hand-drawn elements**: Subtle imperfections for personality
- **Color blocks**: Bold purple and orange used strategically
- **Consistent spacing**: Grid-based, organized layouts

## Brand Voice
- Direct and honest
- Energetic but not overwhelming
- Inclusive and welcoming
- Action-oriented
