<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Swiss Style / International Typographic Style (Modernized)
  **Core Principles**: Clarity, Objectivity, Grid-based precision, Asymmetry
  **Color Philosophy**: Northeastern Red (#CC0000) as a bold accent against a stark, high-contrast black and white background. The red signifies action and passion, while the monochrome base emphasizes professionalism and structure.
  **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric grid layouts with strong vertical and horizontal axes. Use of negative space to guide the eye. Avoid centered text blocks; prefer left-aligned typography for readability and modern feel.
  **Signature Elements**:
    - Large, bold sans-serif typography (Helvetica or similar neo-grotesque)
    - Geometric shapes and lines to separate sections
    - High-contrast photography with red duotone effects
  **Interaction Philosophy**: Precise and snappy. Hover effects that reveal information or shift colors sharply. No unnecessary bounce or fluff.
  **Animation**: Slide-in animations from the sides, sharp transitions, text reveals that mask up.
  **Typography System**:
    - Headings: Inter (Bold/Black), tight tracking
    - Body: Inter (Regular), comfortable line height
    - Monospace accents for technical details
</idea>

  **Core Principles**: Rawness, Boldness, Unapologetic, Function-over-form (stylized)

    - Thick black borders (2px-4px)
    - Drop shadows that are solid black (no blur)
    - Marquee scrolling text for announcements
  **Interaction Philosophy**: Tactile and mechanical. Buttons that depress visibly (transform: translate). Cursor effects.
  **Animation**: Staggered reveals, marquee scrolls, raw cuts between states.
  **Typography System**:
    - Headings: Space Grotesk or similar quirky sans-serif
    - Body: System font or a clean sans-serif

  **Design Movement**: Tech Minimalist / SaaS Aesthetic
  **Core Principles**: Cleanliness, Trust, Scalability, Modernity

    - Rounded corners (large radii)
    - Glassmorphism effects for sticky nav and cards
  **Interaction Philosophy**: Fluid and smooth. Soft fades, gentle scaling on hover.
  **Animation**: Fade-up elements as you scroll. Smooth parallax on background elements.
  **Typography System**:
    - Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans or DM Sans (Geometric, friendly but professional)
    - Body: Inter or Roboto
